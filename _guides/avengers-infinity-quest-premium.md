## About the Table
Avengers: Infinity Quest is a 2020 Stern pinball machine based on the Marvel Cinematic Universe. The Premium model features a rotating disc mechanism, six Infinity Gems to collect through quest modes, and deep rule integration with Avenger character progression. The game emphasizes combo shooting and strategic gem placement for maximum scoring efficiency.

## Skill Shots
**Basic Skill Shot**: Soft plunge past the last one-way gate near the pop bumpers for +3 seconds ball save.

**Super Skill Shot**: Full plunge, then shoot the Tower for +5 seconds ball save (low value, avoid in tournaments).

**Super Secret Skill Shot** (recommended): Full plunge, then immediately shoot the center drop target. Awards +10 seconds ball save plus the currently flashing grid award. This is easier than the other secret skill shot and can award a Portal Lock through tic-tac-toe completion.

**Secret Skill Shot** (hardest): Soft plunge to just before the one-way gate for +10 seconds ball save and one Portal Lock. Difficult but valuable if mastered.

## Early Game Strategy
Start immediately by shooting combos - prioritize left ramp to Captain Marvel, then repeat. The combo system is crucial: 4 combos lights a Portal Lock, 10 combos lights another Portal Lock. Your first 30 seconds should focus on safe ramp shots to build toward these Portal Locks, as they provide playfield multipliers (2x for one lock, 3x for two locks) that make any subsequent mode much more valuable.

If comfortable with the center drop target, use the Super Secret skill shot to potentially gain an early Portal Lock through the computer grid system.

## Mid Game Strategy
**Core Tournament Strategy**: Build toward 2 Portal Locks, then start Reality Gem Quest for quick 100M+ scoring.

1. **Portal Lock Collection**: Continue combo shooting (left ramp to Captain Marvel) until you have 2 Portal Locks banked. Alternative methods include Hawkeye Challenge and computer grid tic-tac-toe completions.

2. **Reality Gem Quest**: Once you have 2 Portal Locks, start Reality Gem Quest (spin disc to light shots, need 8 shots to complete). The Portal Locks give 3x scoring multiplier throughout the quest.

3. **Multiball Stacking**: Keep Thor Multiball on standby (2 hits to the captive ball) before starting Reality Gem. During the gem quest, complete Thor Multiball to maintain the 3x scoring as long as you stay in multiball. Iron Man Multiball can also be stacked at the Tower.

4. **Disc Strategy**: During Reality Gem Quest, spin the disc aggressively to maximize shot multipliers. Captain America and upper loop shots are worth double values during this mode.

## Late Game Strategy
Focus on completing any started modes rather than beginning new complex quests. If you only have 1 Portal Lock by ball 3, still start Reality Gem Quest at 2x scoring to get points on the board. 

Preserve any collected gems between balls - they carry over and maintain their power-ups. If time is running out, shoot for safe ramp combos or work toward completing any lit Combo Jackpots rather than attempting difficult gem quests.

Consider Computer Frenzy Multiball as a late-game bailout - it's easier to achieve and provides decent scoring without complex rules.

## Multiballs
**Thor Multiball**: Hit the Thor captive ball 2-3 times. Can be stacked with gem quests. Strategy is to hit lit shots to progress jackpot values, then shoot the Thor ball for collection.

**Iron Man Multiball**: Lock 3 balls at the Tower (Premium has a physical lock). Best used to extend other modes like Reality Gem Quest, maintaining the 3x Portal Lock scoring.

**Black Order Multiball**: Advanced multiball with phases. Hit lit shots to light the Dr. Strange disc and collect STRANGE letters, then shoot Tower for super jackpot.

**Computer Frenzy**: Easier multiball to start when in trouble - good bailout option for late-game scoring.

## Key Tips
- **Portal Locks are everything**: Always prioritize getting 1-2 Portal Locks before starting any gem quest. They provide crucial scoring multipliers (2x/3x).
- **Reality Gem is the tournament gem**: Easiest to complete (just 8 shots) and pairs perfectly with Portal Lock multipliers for quick 100M+.
- **Combo system**: Left ramp to Captain Marvel is the safest repeatable combo shot. 4, 10, 20 combos award Portal Locks.
- **Gem placement matters**: Place Reality Gem on Captain Marvel spinner or Black Panther for maximum benefit.
- **Stack multiballs**: Keep Thor or Iron Man Multiball ready before starting gem quests to extend the 3x scoring period.
- **Computer Grid**: The tic-tac-toe grid can award Portal Locks - learn the pattern and use Power Gem on drop targets to light multiple squares.
- **Avoid complex early**: Skip Soul Gem Quest and other complex modes in tournament play - focus on the Portal Lock + Reality Gem strategy.
- **Ball save extensions**: All skill shots provide ball save time - crucial for tournament survival.
