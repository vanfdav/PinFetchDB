## About the Table
Banzai Run is a unique 1988 Williams machine designed by Pat Lawlor featuring two playfields - a standard lower playfield and a vertical upper playfield in the backglass with 3 additional flippers. The motorcycle racing theme centers around defeating opponents to access multiball and score the maximum points through cliff jumps and victory laps.

## Skill Shots
Medium plunge into the flashing saucer for the skill shot. This immediately spots a racer and is the safest way to start each ball. The skill shot is relatively easy to execute and provides immediate progress toward accessing the upper playfield.

## Early Game Strategy
Focus on building laps via the ramp immediately - each ramp shot awards laps and increases the captive ball value on the lower playfield. Collect the green rollovers at the top of the table to light Green Machine. Use the top right saucer to spot additional racers and relight the kickback. Avoid the dangerous 1-lap standup targets as they're drain risks with minimal reward.

## Mid Game Strategy
The core strategy involves managing access to the upper playfield through strategic opponent collection. Complete any three colored targets (green rollovers or corresponding arrows) to light access to the upper playfield via the spinner shot. Once on the upper playfield, repeatedly hit the Red Hot cliff jump shot at the very top for 75,000+ points that increase each time. Complete A-B-C targets on the upper playfield to double cliff jump values. Key strategy: leave one opponent lit but undefeated to maintain consistent access to the upper playfield rather than defeating all opponents immediately.

## Late Game Strategy
Continue cliff jump strategy if upper playfield access remains available. If all opponents are defeated, focus on multiball for victory laps worth 250,000 points each after the jackpot. The 25-lap finish line bonus worth 750,000 points becomes crucial in close games. Be conservative with ball control on the upper playfield - make quick, controlled shots and trap up between attempts.

## Multiballs
Lock one ball on the upper playfield after defeating all four opponents to start multiball. During multiball, re-lock one ball in the lower playfield saucer, then get the second ball to the upper playfield within the time limit. Hit the captive ball at the top of the upper playfield for the jackpot. After jackpot collection, every ramp shot awards victory laps worth 250,000 points plus 2 laps toward the finish line bonus. Only one jackpot can be scored per multiball.

## Key Tips
- Cliff jumps start at 75,000+ and increase each time, making them the highest-value repeatable shot
- Hit A-B-C targets on upper playfield to double cliff jump values before going for repeated cliff jumps  
- In tournament ROM settings, cliff jumps may be fixed at 25,000 but are still the best strategy for upper playfield time
- Victory laps after multiball jackpot are worth 250,000 each - prioritize ramp shots post-jackpot
- 25 total laps awards 750,000 finish line bonus and extra ball
- Top right saucer relights kickback and spots racers - use it strategically
- Captive ball on lower playfield increases in value with laps collected (50,000+ per shot after several ramp shots)
- Avoid 1-lap standup targets - they're dangerous drain shots with minimal value compared to ramp shots
- Upper playfield post timing can be adjusted in tournament settings to increase difficulty
