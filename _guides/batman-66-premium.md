## About the Table
Batman 66 Premium is a 2016 Stern pinball machine designed by George Gomez with code by Lyman Sheats. Based on the classic 1960s TV series, it features the spinning Bat-Turn disk, moving Penguin crane, and multiple villain modes. The Premium model includes additional features like the spinning disk and enhanced lighting that make it the preferred tournament version.

## Skill Shots
The standard skill shot involves plunging to the lit standup target for base points. However, the superior skill shot is to **press the left flipper button during plunge, catch the ball on the left flipper, and immediately shoot the scoop**. This "super skill shot" averages 3 million points, significantly better than the standard skill shot. This technique requires practice but is worth mastering for tournament play.

## Early Game Strategy
Start every ball by hitting the **Gordon standup target 3 times** to light 2x shot multipliers across the playfield. This is your foundation for big scoring. Complete the **four bat inlane rollovers** to light Mystery at the top scoop (accessed via hard left orbit shot). Focus on **TV targets** (three targets on the right) to light minor villain modes, and begin working toward locking balls by hitting the **two standups flanking the spinning disk** to light locks on the right orbit.

## Mid Game Strategy
The core tournament strategy revolves around completing **Bookworm and Mr. Freeze minor villains on the same ball** to activate "frozen timers" - a game-breaking feature that makes all timed modes run indefinitely for the rest of your game. After achieving this, focus on **Penguin mode** exclusively, as it's considered the highest-scoring and safest major villain mode. Hit the crane in all 5 positions and collect the super jackpot at the crane scoop. Stack this with **playfield multipliers** (shoot through the spinning disk when the large red siren light is lit) and **shot multipliers** (continue hitting Gordon standup for 3x, 4x, 5x, 6x multipliers).

## Late Game Strategy
With frozen timers active, repeatedly play **Penguin mode** while maintaining high shot multipliers. The mode is relatively safe with predictable ball paths. Use **King Tut** to increase your main channel multiplier to 5x before starting other modes. End each ball by shooting the **left ramp three times then the right ramp** to complete combos for bonus multiplier. Avoid risky shots near the end of balls - the spinner area and hard shots to the upper playfield can be dangerous.

## Multiballs
**Villain Multiball**: Lock 3 balls at the right orbit (light locks by hitting standups beside spinning disk). All shots are lit for jackpots. Hit the **mystery scoop during multiball for add-a-ball** - this is crucial for extending multiball scoring. 

**Shame Multiball**: Complete TV targets, start any minor villain mode, choose "Shame" for 2-ball multiball. Less valuable than Villain Multiball but easier to achieve.

**Holy Multiball**: Access through Batman TV modes. Can be stacked with other scoring features for massive points.

## Key Tips
- **Complete Bookworm then Mr. Freeze on the same ball** for frozen timers - this is the key to billion-point games
- **Gordon standup progression**: 3 hits = 2x multiplier, 4 hits = 3x, 5 hits = 4x, continuing up to 6x shot multipliers
- **Penguin mode is king** in tournament play - safest high-scoring mode with predictable shots
- **Both CAT ramps can be backhanded** with their respective flippers for safer Catwoman mode completion  
- **Use action button** to switch between main channel content (modes/multiball)
- **Playfield multiplier**: Shoot spinning disk when large red siren light is active
- **Mystery awards** light by completing 4 bat inlanes, collect at top scoop via hard left orbit
- **Avoid the atomic pile** unless specifically lit - dangerous shot with limited benefit
- **Complete TV wizard modes** can be stacked with Villain Escape for massive scoring opportunities
