## About the Table
Stern's 2019 Steve Ritchie design featuring a medieval fantasy theme where players battle monsters to reach the Black Knight's castle. The Premium model includes a unique transparent upper playfield with 3-ball lock mechanism, enabling up to 6-ball multiball and additional strategic options not available on the Pro model.

## Skill Shots
Hold left flipper during plunge for super skill shots. Options include the Black Knight center target (recommended for reliability), his flail, shield, or a soft plunge to the top lanes. The center target super skill shot provides the best risk/reward ratio. Regular skill shot lanes at top provide modest scoring and lane completion progress.

## Early Game Strategy
Focus on safe, repeatable shots that feed back to flippers. The left ramp and center ramp are your primary tools - both return to controllable positions. Start by completing WAR lanes at the top (3M base value hurry-ups). Use backhand technique extensively - nearly every shot on the table can be backhanded safely. Avoid the dangerous Black Knight center target unless absolutely necessary.

## Mid Game Strategy
The core strategy revolves around stacking modes with multiball. Light locks by hitting the lock target (right of shield), then backhand the shield scoop to lock balls. With 2 balls locked and a mode ready, the final lock to the center scoop stacks Knight multiball with your mode for massive scoring. On Premium, utilize the upper playfield's "light lock" feature to spot mode shots and increase mode values via the upper loop. The left ramp becomes crucial for building mode values and accessing upper playfield features.

## Late Game Strategy
WAR hurry-ups become extremely valuable late in the game, potentially worth 85M+ on a single collect. Focus on completing W-A-R lanes repeatedly and collecting these hurry-ups during multiball for maximum safety. If you have super jackpots built up, cash them during Knight multiball when ball save is active. Be conservative with dangerous shots unless protected by multiball ball save.

## Multiballs
**Knight Multiball (Main)**: Lock 3 balls at shield scoop. Stack with modes for optimal scoring.  
**Triple Knights Challenge (Premium)**: Lock balls on upper playfield behind raised shield. Can achieve 6-ball multiball when stacked with main multiball.  
**Catapult Multiball (Premium/LE)**: Available through specific sequences, provides additional multiball stacking opportunities.  
**Black Knight 2000 Multiball**: Earned by spelling KNIGHT through good mode/multiball play. Timed wizard-level multiball with significant scoring potential.

## Key Tips
- Most shots are backhandable - use this to maintain control and safety
- Never shoot the Black Knight center target outside of multiball unless required - extremely dangerous
- WAR hurry-ups start at 3M base value and can build to 85M+ - prioritize these heavily
- Stack modes with multiball by having 2 balls locked, get mode ready, then final lock
- Premium: Use upper playfield left ramp strategy to spot mode shots and build values
- Complete drop target banks before starting modes to upgrade with POWER
- Choose shots that feed back to flippers: center ramp, scoops, left ramp
- Speed kills on this table - slow down and trap up frequently
- Light extra balls through completing modes, U-Turn combos, and RANSOM mode
- Super feature jackpot can be collected via magna-save when ball is on ramp wireform
