## About the Table
Harlem Globetrotters On Tour is a 1979 Bally solid state machine featuring the famous basketball team. The table features unique stacked "scissor" flippers on the left, inline drop targets, three spinners, and a bonus-heavy scoring system that rewards consistent shooting and multiplier building.

## Skill Shots
There is no traditional plunge skill shot on this machine. Instead, focus on controlled shooting from the start. The most important early shots are the inline drop targets on the right side, which simultaneously build spinner value, bonus multipliers, and spot GLOBE letters.

## Early Game Strategy
Immediately target the inline drop targets on the right side of the playfield. Each drop target accomplishes three critical objectives: adds 1K to bonus, spots a GLOBE letter, and increases the left spinner value (progressing through 100-200-1000-2000 points per spin). Hit at least 3 drops to get the spinner to 1K value before focusing on spinner shots. The right standup target should be hit early to open the right outlane gate for ball save insurance.

## Mid Game Strategy
Once spinner value is built up, alternate between hitting the left spinner (worth up to 2K per spin) and completing the inline drops for bonus multipliers (2X-3X-5X). The left spinner becomes the primary money shot when valued at 1K or 2K per spin. Collect GLOBE letters either from drops or the top saucer - completing GLOBE scores 50K and lights the left outlane for 25K. Work on completing the left standup bank (5 targets) to advance the Slam Dunk awards and eventually light extra ball at the lower right standup target.

## Late Game Strategy
Focus on reaching Super Bonus thresholds (typically 20K minimum, can advance to 40K+) as this carries over between balls and is the key to big tournament scores. Prioritize safe spinner shots over risky saucer attempts late in the ball. The center spinners advance bonus by 1K for every 5 spins, making them valuable for bonus building when the left spinner isn't available.

## Multiballs
This is a single-ball machine with no multiball features. Tournament strategy focuses entirely on bonus accumulation and multiplier management.

## Key Tips
- **Inline drops are everything**: Hit them repeatedly to build spinner value (up to 2K/spin) and bonus multipliers (up to 5X)
- **Left spinner is the money shot**: When valued at 1K-2K per spin, this becomes your primary scoring opportunity
- **Avoid the scissor gap**: The most dangerous drain on this machine is between the two left flippers - never try to trap between them
- **Super Bonus carries over**: Reaching 20K+ bonus carries to the next ball, making it essential for competitive scores
- **Right outlane gate**: Hit the right standup target to open the gate before attempting risky shots
- **GLOBE completion**: Worth 50K each time and lights left outlane for 25K - steady points that add up
- **Bonus multipliers don't carry**: Unlike Super Bonus, multipliers reset each ball, so cash them in
- **Center spinners**: Advance bonus by 1K every 5 spins - useful when left spinner isn't accessible
