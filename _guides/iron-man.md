## About the Table
Iron Man is a 2010 Stern pinball machine based on the Marvel superhero and featuring themes from the Iron Man movies. The game features three main multiballs (Iron Monger, War Machine, Whiplash), ramp-focused scoring, and a challenging wizard mode called "Do or Die Multiball." The layout includes left and right ramps, spinner shots, and a prominent Iron Monger toy in the center of the playfield.

## Skill Shots
**Standard Skill Shot**: Plunge for the flashing top lane rollover for basic skill shot points.

**Super Skill Shot**: Hold the left flipper button and medium plunge to hit the flashing top lane. This keeps the orbit up-post down, making the shot more dangerous but potentially more valuable. The San Diego Pinball Wiki notes this can be safer to short plunge instead for consistency.

For tournament play, the standard skill shot is recommended as it's safer and more consistent. The super skill shot adds unnecessary risk without significantly higher reward.

## Early Game Strategy
Focus on safe, repeatable scoring in the first 30 seconds:
- Shoot the left and right ramps to build Bogey mode (need 4 total ramp shots)
- Collect THRUST letters on inlanes/outlanes to activate kickbacks - this is crucial for ball control
- Hit SAVE targets at ramp entrances to activate ball save
- Begin working on Iron Man targets to enable scoring multipliers (Fast Scoring makes all switches worth at least 10K)

Avoid the dangerous Iron Monger bash toy early unless you're very confident - the magnetic interference makes it unpredictable.

## Mid Game Strategy
**Primary Scoring Focus - Bogey Mode**: This is your most reliable point manufacturing method. Shoot any combination of left/right ramps 4 times to qualify, then shoot lit shots during the mode. Shooting ramps in succession (left-right-left-right) doubles the points. This mode can be stacked with multiballs for maximum effectiveness.

**Multiball Preparation**:
- **War Machine MB**: Hit all 4 drone targets, then shoot War Machine kicker
- **Whiplash MB**: Hit Whiplash target 5 times (reduced to +1 hit for subsequent starts in newer code)
- **Iron Monger MB**: Shoot spinners then bash the center toy (hit from sides to minimize magnetic interference)

**Iron Man Target Strategy**: Complete I-R-O-N M-A-N targets to activate scoring multipliers:
- Fast Scoring: All switches worth minimum 10K
- Double Scoring: All scores doubled
- Iron Man Scoring: Targets worth 1M hurry-up

## Late Game Strategy
If approaching Do or Die qualification, prioritize completing multiball super jackpots rather than starting new ones. For end-of-ball bonus considerations, focus on completing any lit modes rather than starting new features.

**Conservative Play**: When ahead, avoid the dangerous Iron Monger shot and focus on safe ramp shots for Bogey scoring. The game is quite draining, so ball control is paramount.

**Aggressive Play**: When behind, stack War Machine multiball with another multiball (War Machine must be started first) and pound the ramps during the stack.

## Multiballs

**War Machine Multiball**: Start by hitting 4 drone targets, then shoot War Machine kicker. Features unlimited add-a-ball for 20 seconds on the War Machine kicker. Can be stacked with other multiballs if started first. Focus on ramps for jackpots.

**Whiplash Multiball**: Hit Whiplash target 5 times to start. Two-ball multiball focused on ramp shots. Cannot be stacked with other multiballs if started first.

**Iron Monger Multiball**: Shoot spinners then bash Iron Monger toy. Three-ball multiball. Hit Iron Monger from the sides to avoid magnetic interference. Cannot be stacked if started first.

**Do or Die Multiball** (Wizard Mode): Requires completing Iron Man 3 times, and collecting super jackpots from all three multiballs, plus hitting drones 4 times. Features a 35M hurry-up (up to 70M with 2X scoring active) - shoot center lane to start countdown, then center again to collect.

## Key Tips
- **Best scoring strategy**: Get Bogey mode going early and stack it with multiballs, especially War Machine
- **Iron Man 2X scoring exploit**: Start Iron Man 2X scoring just before starting Do or Die for double hurry-up value (up to 70M)
- **Dangerous shots to avoid**: Iron Monger bash toy (magnetic interference), center spinner shots
- **Safe money shots**: Left and right ramps are the most reliable scoring
- **Multiball stacking**: War Machine can stack with others if started first - use this for maximum scoring
- **Nudging**: Game is quite draining - save nudges for critical moments and focus on clean flipper technique
- **Mode preparation**: Get each mode 1-2 shots away from starting before playing any of them to make Do or Die easier to achieve
- **Bogey ramp balance**: On hard settings, once you shoot a ramp 4 times it stops scoring until Bogey starts, so balance left/right ramp shots
