## About the Table
James Bond 007 (60th Anniversary LE) is a 2023 Stern pinball machine designed by Keith Elwin celebrating 60 years of the Bond franchise. The game features a retro design with mechanical score reels, four optical spinners, and a unique scoring system capped at 49,999 points before "Over The Top" lighting. The playfield includes MI6 drop targets, inline drops for multiball locks, and Oddjob's spinning hat mechanism.

## Skill Shots
**Normal Skill Shot**: Plunge to the flashing lane for 100 points. No lane change available, so timing is critical.

**Super Skill Shot**: Hold left flipper, full plunge, and immediately shoot the inline drop targets. This automatically lights the lock and awards 150 points, making it the highest-value skill shot option.

**Q Skill Shot**: Soft plunge to right flipper and hit the Q target for 150 points, though this is riskier than the super skill shot.

The super skill shot is the optimal choice as it provides the highest scoring and instantly progresses toward Mission Multiball.

## Early Game Strategy
Start every ball with the super skill shot to light the lock immediately. Your first 30 seconds should focus on building bonus multiplier since end-of-ball bonus can reach 3000 points (1000x3). Hit the first inline drop to light the inlanes for Bonus Advance, then work on completing the ABC targets to advance bonus multiplier. The Q target can spot one ABC letter if needed. Avoid risky shots and focus on safe, repeatable scoring through the spinners and basic target completion.

## Mid Game Strategy
Prioritize villain modes by completing the center MI6 bank. Each mode corresponds to specific table features (spinners, loops) and shows remaining shots on the display. The key tournament strategy is to complete MI6 two more times during any active mode to start **double mode scoring** - this can be more lucrative than multiball according to community tips. Modes award a 1000-point bonus for first-time completion. Use the hat spinner to cycle through available villain modes and select the easiest one for your skill level. Stack modes with multiballs when possible since everything stacks in this game.

## Late Game Strategy
Focus heavily on bonus collection if your multiplier is built up. With maxed bonus (1000x3 = 3000 points), this represents massive scoring potential. Hit the second inline drop to light Bonus Collect on the M target, which feeds the plunger lane. If you have the Jet Pack gadget, your bonus carries over to the next ball (but not bonus multiplier). When bonus is maxed and you're confident in your control, consider taking the bonus collect shot. Otherwise, focus on safe villain mode completions and avoid the dangerous outlanes.

## Multiballs

**Mission Multiball**: Lock a ball behind the inline drops. If you have Spy Glasses gadget, use the center button to instantly complete the drops. This 2-ball multiball becomes 3-ball when you lock both balls in the left and right saucers. Re-lock balls and shoot the captive ball for 500-point jackpots (1000 if two balls re-locked), then hit the M target for 1500-point super jackpots.

**Villain Multiball**: Complete all 6 villain modes to start. Hit spinners for jackpots during this multiball.

**Gadget Multiball**: Activated by the X-Ray Glasses gadget (4th gadget). Shoot the hat spinner for jackpots.

**Bond Multiball**: Collect 6 Bonds by completing BOND targets or starting villain modes. This is a switch-based multiball where you shoot various shots and spinners for jackpots.

## Key Tips
- **Bonus multiplier is king**: ABC completion for 3x bonus multiplier can be worth 1000+ points per shot when bonus is built
- **Double mode scoring**: Complete MI6 twice more during any active mode for doubled scoring - often better than multiball jackpots
- **Super skill shot every ball**: Hold left flipper, full plunge, shoot inline drops for instant lock + 150 points
- **Stack everything**: All features stack - use multiball time to complete MI6 and BOND targets safely
- **Gadget priority**: Get Jet Pack first (bonus carryover), then Spy Glasses (instant lock button)
- **Avoid outlanes**: Use nudging aggressively as there's limited outlane ball save (Hovercraft gadget only)
- **Spinner strategy**: The four optical spinners are key scoring during multiballs and some modes
- **Mode intel**: Check the LCD screen inside the playfield for mode directions since the main display doesn't overlay instructions
