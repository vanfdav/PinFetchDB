## About the Table
Joker Poker is a Gottlieb electromechanical pinball machine from 1978 featuring a poker theme with 15 drop targets arranged in five poker hands (10, Jacks, Queens, Kings, and Aces+Joker). The game emphasizes completing specific drop target banks on each ball to light the 5X bonus multiplier, making it crucial to understand the changing objectives throughout each game.

## Skill Shots
Plunge for the **A or C lanes** at the top of the playfield. The B lane can be spotted by the inlanes, while the A and C lanes are only available on the outlanes, making them more valuable to collect during the skill shot. With Pascal board modifications, hitting the A or C skill shot can be worth 10,000 points.

## Early Game Strategy
Focus immediately on the **ball-specific drop target objective** for the 5X bonus multiplier:
- **Ball 1**: Hit the single "10" drop target (easiest objective)
- **Ball 2**: Complete all three Queen drop targets  
- **Ball 3**: Complete the Aces+Joker bank (5 targets)

On 5-ball games, the progression is 10 → Jacks → Queens → Kings → Aces+Joker. Getting the 5X bonus lit should be your absolute priority before pursuing other scoring opportunities.

## Mid Game Strategy
After lighting 5X bonus, focus on the **Queens bank as your primary scoring strategy**. The first complete set of Queens is worth 3,000 points, but each individual Queen target hit after completing the first set is worth **5,000 points each**, plus another 5,000 for completing subsequent Queen banks. This is the safest and most repeatable high-value shot sequence.

Complete other drop target banks to build bonus: 10s = 1,000 bonus, Jacks = 2,000, Queens = 3,000, Kings = 4,000, Aces+Joker = 5,000. Maximum base bonus is 15,000 points, which becomes 30,000 with the 2X multiplier or 75,000 with 5X.

## Late Game Strategy
**Save the Kings bank for last** — these are the hardest targets to hit consistently. If you have 5X bonus lit and substantial bonus built up, play conservatively to preserve the ball and collect the multiplied bonus. Feed the ball to the upper playfield toward the Ace bank when possible, as this is generally the safest area to play from.

## Multiballs
Joker Poker does not feature multiball modes. This is a single-ball electromechanical game focused on drop target completion and bonus collection.

## Key Tips
- **Queens are your money shot**: After the first completion, each Queen hit is worth 5,000 points and they're the safest targets
- **Upper playfield is safer**: Feed the ball up top toward the Aces rather than shooting dangerous lower targets
- **Drop targets don't reset**: Unlike solid state versions, completed banks stay down for the entire ball
- **Target linking**: The 10 and Jacks reset together — both banks must be completed before either resets
- **Bonus multiplier priority**: Always get your ball-specific objective first before going for other scoring
- **Avoid the far-left Ace**: This target is extremely difficult to hit from any flipper position
- **Pascal board scoring**: If installed, Special can be worth 50,000 points and Extra Ball 10,000-20,000 points
- **5-ball setup recommended**: The progression of objectives (10→J→Q→K→A+Joker) creates better strategic depth than 3-ball
