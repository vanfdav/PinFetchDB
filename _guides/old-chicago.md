## About the Table
Old Chicago is a 1976 electromechanical Bally pinball machine featuring a classic EM layout with five pop bumpers, a left spinner, bank of five drop targets, two saucers, and center bonus collection. The game revolves around building bonus through the "OLD CHICAGO" letters and collecting it strategically for maximum scoring.

## Skill Shots
The skill shot involves hitting the lit top lane for **3,000 points and two bonus advances**. The top rollover lanes toggle the lit lane when hit by the playfield rubbers at the top. While the skill shot provides decent value, focus more on nudging carefully to hit the lit lane rather than relying on precise plunging, as the EM nature makes consistent skill shots challenging.

## Early Game Strategy
Start each ball by immediately working the **left spinner to build bonus letters** in "OLD CHICAGO". Each spinner hit advances the bonus ladder. Focus on safe, controlled spinner shots rather than wild rips. Once you've built some bonus (aim for at least 4-5 letters), test shots to the **center saucer** to collect bonus. The center saucer has side rails that make it moderately dangerous, so only attempt collection shots when the risk/reward ratio favors it.

## Mid Game Strategy
The core strategy is the **spinner-to-saucer loop**: hit the left spinner to build bonus, then collect at the center saucer, and repeat. Only shoot the center saucer when its value reaches **6,000 points or higher** to maximize efficiency. When trapped up on the left flipper with low bonus, target the **drop targets or upper right saucer** for immediate points rather than risky bonus collection attempts. The five-bank of drop targets can provide solid scoring and are generally safer than the center saucer.

## Late Game Strategy
On balls 3 and 5, **bonus is doubled**, making bonus collection extremely valuable. Prioritize building maximum bonus on these balls before collecting. Be extra cautious with the center saucer shot as the doubled bonus makes it worth the risk. Avoid the dangerous areas around the pop bumpers late in the ball when preservation is key. The left spinner area can send balls over to the center bumpers unpredictably.

## Multiballs
Old Chicago is a single-ball EM game with no multiball features.

## Key Tips
- **Primary strategy**: Hit left spinner to build "OLD CHICAGO" letters, collect at center saucer when value is 6K+
- **Bonus doubles on balls 3 and 5** - maximize bonus building on these balls
- **Top lanes worth 3,000 points and two bonus advances** - nudge carefully to hit the lit lane
- **Drop targets light special** (left outlane) and **extra ball** (right outlane) when completed
- **Avoid center saucer side rails** - hit cleanly or risk dangerous deflections
- **Left spinner shots can fall back** if they don't make it to the top - be ready for ball control
- **Upper right saucer scores 5 G's and bonus** - good alternative when left flipper is trapped
- **Center pop bumper only scores 10 points** while side bumpers score 100 - avoid the center one
- **Watch for ball return issues** - some machines have switch problems that affect ball counting
