## About the Table
Pinball Pool is a 1979 Gottlieb System 1 solid state machine with a pool/billiards theme. The playfield features 14 drop targets arranged in two 7-target banks, a captive 8-ball in the center, two saucer holes, and the signature "Remotrip" feature where hitting certain targets also drops the opposing target. This creates a strategic game focused on completing the full rack of 15 balls and maximizing bonus multipliers.

## Skill Shots
There is no traditional skill shot on Pinball Pool. The plunger sends the ball through top lanes labeled A-B-C, with the goal being to complete all three letters to light extra ball at the 8-ball shot. The B lane appears to be the most common result from a standard plunge, so focus on hitting A and C lanes during regular play. The top lanes provide minimal scoring value, so prioritize getting the ball safely into play over precision aiming.

## Early Game Strategy
Immediately focus on building your bonus multiplier by shooting the two saucer holes. Each saucer shot increases your bonus multiplier by 1X, capping at 5X after 4 shots. This is critical because bonus scoring is 1,000 points per pool ball (15,000 for a complete rack) times your multiplier - potentially 75,000 points with 5X multiplier. The saucers are relatively safe shots accessible from both flippers, making this the ideal early game strategy before attempting the more dangerous drop target shots.

## Mid Game Strategy
Once you have 5X bonus multiplier, focus on completing the 15 pool balls: 14 drop targets plus the center captive 8-ball. The key feature is that hitting even-numbered drop targets (2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12, 14) also drops the corresponding odd-numbered target on the opposite side. This "Remotrip" feature can be adjusted on some machines, so observe or ask about settings before play. Complete all 15 targets to light the saucers for bonus collect, then shoot a saucer to collect your bonus and reset all targets for another cycle.

## Late Game Strategy
With high bonus multipliers established, each complete rack becomes worth 75,000 points. Focus on safe, controlled play to complete target sequences and collect bonuses multiple times per ball. The bonus multiplier carries over throughout the ball but resets to 1X on the next ball, so maximize collections before draining. Avoid risky shots late in the ball - side drains award 5,000 points and advance bonus multiplier (if not at 5X), but this is minimal compared to completing full cycles.

## Multiballs
Pinball Pool does not feature multiball play. This is a single-ball game focused on methodical target completion and bonus collection.

## Key Tips
- **Prioritize saucer shots first**: Get to 5X bonus multiplier before focusing on drop targets
- **Learn the Remotrip pattern**: Even-numbered targets drop their odd-numbered counterparts
- **Target bank strategy**: Focus on one bank at a time rather than randomly hitting targets
- **Captive 8-ball timing**: Save the center 8-ball shot for last to complete the 15th ball
- **Side drains have value**: Worth 5K and bonus multiplier advance, better than center drain
- **A-B-C completion**: Lights extra ball at 8-ball shot, but often disabled in tournament play
- **Bonus preservation**: Multiplier stays active for the entire ball, so multiple collections are possible
- **Saucer safety**: These are the safest high-value shots on the playfield from either flipper
- **Avoid top playfield**: Bumpers and upper areas offer minimal scoring compared to main cycle
- **Watch for tilt sensitivity**: Older Gottlieb machines can be tilt-sensitive during aggressive play
