## About the Table
Rick and Morty is a 2019 Spooky Pinball release designed by Scott Danesi with rules by Bowen Kerins. The machine features dimensional travel mechanics that randomly alter scoring and gameplay, multiple adventures (modes), and a unique pop bumper replacing the traditional left slingshot. The game emphasizes collecting Megaseeds for end-of-ball bonus multipliers and features the innovative Flooble Crank mechanism on the right ramp.

## Skill Shots
Two main skill shots are available on the plunge:
- **Noob Noob Target**: Worth 200,000 points - easier and safer option
- **Garage Shot**: Worth 400,000 points - higher risk but double the reward
- **Multiplier Strategy**: Hit the inner orbit (spinner shot) before collecting either skill shot to multiply the value significantly

The garage shot offers the best risk/reward ratio for tournament play, especially when multiplied by the inner orbit first.

## Early Game Strategy
Focus on safe, repeatable scoring in the first 30 seconds:
- Always attempt the garage skill shot for 400,000 points
- Hit the inner orbit before skill shots when possible to multiply values
- Begin working toward multiball by hitting the Flooble Crank (left post on right ramp) to light locks
- Start collecting Megaseeds early as they provide 5% bonus multiplier each
- Avoid dangerous shots like the left outlane area until ball control is established

## Mid Game Strategy
The core scoring revolves around Adventures (modes) and multiball stacking:
- **Adventures**: Six different adventures available, each with unique scoring opportunities
- **Dimension Changes**: Occur randomly during play - garage shot changes dimension for 5 lit Megaseeds, right orbit only lights 3
- **Megaseed Collection**: Each collected Megaseed adds 5% to end-of-ball bonus (based on ball score)
- **Multiball Setup**: Hit Flooble Crank 3 times to light locks, then lock balls in the horseshoe
- **Scoring Multipliers**: Different dimensions can multiply specific shots (ramps 2x, spinner 3x, etc.)

## Late Game Strategy
End-of-ball bonus is crucial and based on ball performance:
- **Megaseed Focus**: Prioritize collecting Megaseeds as each adds 5% to your total ball bonus
- **Conservative Play**: The end-of-ball bonus system rewards sustained scoring over risky shots
- **Dimension Management**: Use garage shot strategically to access favorable scoring dimensions
- **Avoid Tilting**: The bonus system makes tilt warnings particularly costly

## Multiballs
**Gromflomite Battle Multiball**:
- Light locks by hitting Flooble Crank 3 times
- Lock balls in horseshoe
- Start multiball by shooting center scoop when two balls are locked
- **Key Strategy**: Hit Flooble Crank 3 times during multiball to add a ball + 10 second ball save (only works once per multiball)
- Focus on keeping balls alive rather than aggressive scoring during multiball

## Key Tips
- **Flooble Crank Add-a-Ball**: During multiball, hit the Flooble Crank 3 times for extra ball and extended ball save - this is a one-time opportunity per multiball
- **Skill Shot Multiplier**: Always try to hit inner orbit before collecting skill shots to multiply their value
- **Megaseed Tracking**: Your score's last two digits show current Megaseed count - aim for high collection
- **Dimension Awareness**: Pay attention to dimension changes as they can dramatically affect scoring values
- **Garage vs Right Orbit**: Garage shot gives 5 lit Megaseeds for dimension change vs only 3 from right orbit
- **Pop Bumper Difference**: The left side has a pop bumper instead of traditional slingshot - adjust nudging accordingly
- **Anti-Gravity Magnet**: Second flipper button on right side activates magnet above right inlane (must be charged first by hitting wireform)
- **Bonus Optimization**: End-of-ball bonus is percentage-based on ball score, making Megaseed collection extremely valuable
