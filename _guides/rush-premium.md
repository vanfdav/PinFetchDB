## About the Table
Rush Premium is a 2022 Stern pinball machine based on the legendary Canadian rock band Rush. The table features a unique electromagnetic Time Machine that serves as the central scoring feature, along with a complex system of albums, modes, and multiballs. The Premium model includes a motorized ramp system and subway ball lock for Far Cry Multiball.

## Skill Shots
Short plunge to the upper flipper for skill shot opportunities. The side scoop (left VUK) awards the highest skill shot value. For maximum value, shoot the inner loop first, then immediately follow with either the side ramp or side scoop for a super skill shot worth double value. Each successful skill shot awards additional ball save time, which is crucial on tournament setups.

## Early Game Strategy
Focus on building toward multiball opportunities and collecting albums for "The Weapon" feature. Start by shooting flashing records to lock in colors - prioritize Working Man (orange) and Big Money (pink) as these stack well with multiball. Complete RUSH standup targets early to light outlane ball save and qualify Bastille Day hurry-up. Hit the spinner and upper loop to build bonus multiplier. Avoid risky shots like the dead-end lane unless specifically needed.

## Mid Game Strategy
The core tournament strategy revolves around mode/multiball stacking. Start a mode first, then immediately work toward starting a multiball during that mode. Working Man (orange) and Big Money (pink) modes are ideal for stacking because their shots remain active during multiball. Use the action button to divert ramps strategically - a slowly strobing flasher indicates which side ramps will feed to. Focus on Far Cry Multiball (hit ramp standups to light locks at side scoop) and Freewill Multiball (complete drop targets to collect instruments). Save "The Weapon" usage for when you have both a mode and multiball active for maximum effectiveness.

## Late Game Strategy
Prioritize safe, high-value shots over risky completions. The spinner shot is generally safe and builds bonus multiplier. If you have The Weapon available (button flashing blue or green), save it for a crucial moment when you can spot all shots in a mode during multiball. Focus on controlled play rather than trying to complete every album or mode. The upper loop is a safe shot that collects bonus multiplier and combo bonuses.

## Multiballs
- **Far Cry Multiball**: Hit ramp standups to light locks, then lock at side scoop. Premium model locks always kick to bottom when one ball is locked. Shoot jackpots quickly, focus on side ramp for super jackpot combos.
- **Freewill Multiball**: Complete drop targets to collect instruments and light multiball at the standup behind drops. Hit captive ball to increase jackpot values. Add-a-ball by hitting the standup again.
- **Time Machine Multiballs**: Three variants (Subdivisions, Fly by Night, Red Barchetta) selected by shooting dead-end lane. Subdivisions is most common in tournament play.
- **Headlong Flight Multiball**: Hit pop bumpers to advance clock to midnight. This multiball can stack with others - start another multiball first, then trigger this one.

## Key Tips
- Use The Weapon strategically when both mode and multiball are active - it spots all shots in single-ball modes (blue button) or adds a ball in multiball (green button)
- Records can be collected anytime outside of modes, even during multiball, for ongoing scoring
- Shooting 6 records resets non-locked colors while keeping matched ones for more shot opportunities
- Rainbow records appear after completing modes and provide 2x scoring during subsequent modes of that color
- The Time Machine always feeds the upper flipper, making it safer for ball control
- Right orbit shot can be dangerous - may drain center if hit too clean without nudging
- Instrument boosts last 120 seconds: Guitar boosts spinner, Bass boosts combos, Drums boosts pops
- Complete 1-2-3 combos (flashing inserts) to light combo jackpots at Time Machine
