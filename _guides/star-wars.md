## About the Table
Star Wars is a 1992 Data East pinball machine based on the original trilogy. The game centers around the Death Star target that rotates and opens, a center ramp for major scoring, and various modes representing iconic movie scenes. Key strategy varies significantly depending on ROM version, with older versions heavily favoring ramp scoring.

## Skill Shots
The skill shot involves timing the plunger trigger to hit a moving TIE Fighter target. **Study the TIE Fighter pattern and fire before it reaches the crosshairs**. Listen for the whooshing sounds - they come in pairs, and you should hit the trigger right when the second whoosh starts. On balls 2 and 3, rapidly pressing the launch button as your turn starts guarantees the skill shot will be made.

## Early Game Strategy
**Check the ROM version immediately** by watching startup display or testing ramp interval awards (1M = new ROM, higher values = old ROM). On old ROM versions, shoot nothing but the center ramp repeatedly for massive scoring. On new ROM versions, focus on setting up multiball by hitting drop targets to relight the left orbit kickback, then working toward Death Star multiball. Complete drop targets three times to light Hyperspace on the ramp.

## Mid Game Strategy
**Strategy depends entirely on ROM version**:

**Old ROM (pre-1.04)**: Shoot the center ramp exclusively. Ramp awards 3M for 3 shots, 7M for 7 shots, then 14M, 21M, etc. Each ramp shot spots a STAR WARS letter (10M when completed). This is the dominant strategy.

**New ROM (1.04+)**: Focus on multiball scoring. Hit the Death Star repeatedly to open it, then land a ball inside to start Tri-Ball. During multiball, hit the Death Star followed by the center ramp to collect jackpots starting at 10M, with each Death Star hit adding 1M to the jackpot value. When Yoda is lit (right lane next to Death Star), prioritize this shot for excellent rewards including 50M Death Star hurry-ups.

## Late Game Strategy
**Replay Score Bonus**: Watch for reaching the replay score - both orbits will light for a few seconds to collect 25M. **Ball Save Awareness**: On newer ROM versions, you get a 10-second ball save, but it turns off after hitting two switches, so play carefully early in each ball. When the ball is heading toward danger, prioritize nudging and ball control over risky shots.

## Multiballs
**Tri-Ball (Death Star Multiball)**: Bash the Death Star repeatedly until it opens, then land a ball inside. Score jackpots by hitting Death Star followed by the center ramp. **Quick multiball tip**: Once all orange Death Star lights are lit, push the launch button to open it for a few seconds, saving you additional shots. Focus on keeping balls alive rather than going for dangerous super jackpots.

## Key Tips
- **ROM Version Check**: New ROM shows 1M interval awards on ramp; old ROM shows much higher values
- **Safe Ramp Shot**: Center ramp can be hit with either flipper and is the safest repeatable shot
- **Kickback Strategy**: Relight left kickback by hitting left orbit through pop bumpers (light orbit by completing drop targets first)  
- **Dead Pass**: Kickouts from right scoop can often be dead passed to right flipper
- **Stormtrooper Bonus**: When Stormtroopers appear on screen, rapidly press fire button on plunger for points
- **Dangerous Areas**: Right outlane drains are common - the faster the ball goes down the right outlane, the more likely it drains
- **Force Awards**: Hit the left orbit lane when lit to collect Force awards and relight features
