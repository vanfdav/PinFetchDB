## About the Table
The Shadow is a 1994 Bally pinball machine based on the pulp hero and movie. It features player-controlled diverters on both ramps, a magnetic ball lock system, and a unique upper "Battlefield" mini-playfield. The game is widely regarded as an excellent tournament machine due to its emphasis on precise shot-making and balanced scoring opportunities.

## Skill Shots
After plunging, shoot the left ramp for the skill shot award. The diverters determine the type of award: the left diverter gives points while the right diverter provides random awards like bonus multiplier, lighting Khan Multiball, or Vengeance. **For tournament play, aim for the right diverter's random awards** as these provide more strategic value than raw points. Vengeance is particularly valuable as bonus multipliers don't affect the lucrative mode points awarded at end of ball.

## Early Game Strategy
**Prioritize spelling KHAN at the return lanes** to increase bonus multiplier and light Khan Multiball at the side saucer. Focus on safe shots to build up the Shadow Loops combo - the **left orbit to upper loop 2-way combo starts at 10 million and increases by 3 million each time**, with values carrying ball-to-ball. This is one of the safest high-value repeatable shots. Avoid risky ramp shots early unless going for specific awards.

## Mid Game Strategy
The core tournament strategy revolves around **completing the four requirements for Final Battle**: Shadow Multiball, Khan Multiball, Battlefield completion, and defeating Khan. **Shadow Loops combos are your bread and butter** - the left orbit to side loop sequence is relatively safe and builds substantial value. For Vengeance, **shoot the four rings around the ramp diverters** (ramps worth 5M, 7M, etc.). Completing all four rings awards 50M, and **comboing all four rings in a row awards 100M**. The **left loop to side loop to Khan scoop combo** increases in value with repetition.

## Late Game Strategy
Mode points are awarded during end-of-ball bonus, so completed scenes provide significant value even if the ball drains. **Focus on completing started modes rather than starting new ones** late in the ball. The Secret Passage (slow orbit shots that dribble to the side loop) awards 5M safely. Trap up and be conservative when close to completing multiball locks or mode objectives, as **ball times tend to be short on this machine** due to aggressive slingshot action.

## Multiballs
**Shadow Multiball**: Lock three balls at the magnetic Sanctum lock. During multiball, shoot arrows for jackpots starting at 10M and increasing by 1M each (up to 30M total). All arrows relight after completion.

**Khan Multiball**: Spell KHAN to light at side saucer. Shoot all major shots for jackpots. The side saucer can be hit directly with the upper right flipper or sometimes by lucky bounces.

**Final Battle**: The ultimate 5-ball multiball requiring completion of both multiballs, Battlefield, and defeating Khan. **Score 10M per shot to every playfield area, with a massive 1 billion point bonus for hitting them all**. Focus on keeping balls alive and systematically hitting each required shot.

## Key Tips
- **Left orbit to upper loop combo** - safest repeatable big points, starts at 10M and increases by 3M each time
- **Pull the trigger during Video Mode** for a "Smart Bomb" that destroys all phurbas (works once per video mode)
- **Pull trigger at the three scoops** to activate secret "Laughing Mode" for additional scoring
- **During Farley Claymore mode**, shoot the ball up to the Battlefield as all hits there count toward completion
- **Vengeance 4-ring combo** - 100M for hitting all four in sequence, safer than individual ramp shots
- **Side saucer shot** is extremely difficult - use upper right flipper or set up bounces
- **Secret Passage** - 5M for slow orbit shots that dribble to side loop
- **Avoid the ramps** unless specifically needed - they're dangerous and the diverters can send balls to outlanes
- **Tournament grand champion scores typically range 2-3 billion**, with A-division players around 1 billion
