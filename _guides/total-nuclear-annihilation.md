## About the Table
Total Nuclear Annihilation is a 2017 Spooky Pinball machine designed by Scott Danesi with a retro-futuristic 1980s theme centered around nuclear reactor destruction. The game features three inline drop targets with ball locking capability, two scoops, and scoring that multiplies during multiball based on the number of balls in play (2x with 2 balls, 3x with 3 balls).

## Skill Shots
**Best skill shot**: Soft plunge to hit the flashing CORE rollover on the upper playfield. This awards 100K points plus starts the reactor automatically. The CORE letter position can be changed with the flippers before plunging - select your target before launching.

**Alternative**: Move the letter position for a 50K skill shot plus ball save activation. The hands-free skill shot (100K + reactor start) offers the best risk/reward ratio for tournament play.

## Early Game Strategy
**First 30 seconds priority**: Execute the skill shot to start a reactor immediately, then focus on filling the grid by hitting the center drop targets. Use the short plunge technique - soft plunge to CORE lanes which feed the upper playfield, then live catch on the right flipper for controlled play. Avoid left flipper shots early as they're more dangerous. Work on locking balls in the inline drop targets via backhand shots from the right flipper.

## Mid Game Strategy
**Primary strategy**: Start multiball as quickly as possible and maintain it. Lock two balls by shooting the inline drop targets, then hit the front standup target to start multiball. During multiball, all playfield values multiply by the number of balls in play (2x or 3x). Focus on shooting the drop targets for jackpots (jackpot → double → triple → super jackpot sequence). The scoop behind the drop targets awards super jackpots plus add-a-ball, extending multiball.

**Reactor scoring**: Try to destroy reactors during multiball for multiplied reactor points. Hit the center grid targets during multiball to heat reactors, then shoot the upper playfield pop bumper to destroy them for massive points.

## Late Game Strategy
Work the SAVE letters in the inlanes/outlanes - completing three SAVE letters lights one flashing. Roll over the flashing lane just before draining to activate ball save. Use this strategically when multiball ends or when attempting risky reactor destruction shots. If ahead, play more conservatively and focus on safe multiball starts rather than risky single-ball reactor shots.

## Multiballs
**Primary Multiball**: Lock balls in the three inline drop targets, then shoot the front standup to release. During multiball, shoot the drop targets for escalating jackpots. The sequence is: Jackpot → Double Jackpot → Triple Jackpot → Super Jackpot (awarded via scoop behind drops + add-a-ball).

**Key multiball tips**: Jackpot values decrease by 45% on the second multiball start per reactor, and another 45% on the third start (down to 10% of original value). Using up a ball save while in multiball restarts the multiball timer, extending play time.

## Key Tips
- **Multiball focus**: "Multiball all day" - this is the primary scoring strategy as all values multiply by balls in play
- **Safe drop target approach**: Shoot drop targets from the right side between targets for safer, more effective targeting
- **Ball save exploitation**: Use frequent ball save opportunities to plunge into CORE lanes or hit grid targets
- **Dangerous shots**: Only use multiball to hit the center grid targets - single ball attempts have high center drain risk
- **Backhand technique**: The inline drop targets are easily backhandable from the right flipper for safer lock shots
- **Reactor timing**: Destroy reactors during 3-ball multiball for triple-multiplied reactor scores
- **Add-a-ball strategy**: Get super jackpots (scoop behind drops) to add balls and extend multiball
- **Nudging**: Be aggressive with nudging to keep balls in play during multiball - the scoring multipliers make every ball valuable
