## About the Table
X's & O's is a 1983 Bally solid-state machine with a tic-tac-toe theme. This early SS game features drop targets, a skill shot, and a unique tic-tac-toe grid in the center playfield that lights up X's and O's as you complete patterns. The machine plays fast with a standard-sized playfield and emphasizes quick, precise shooting.

## Skill Shots
The skill shot is **crucial** and worth 100K when executed properly. **Short plunge** so the ball enters below the upper flipper without hitting the switch in the plunger lane. This shot is on a **10-second timer** from when the ball is fed to the shooter lane, so step up quickly to your turn and be ready to plunge immediately. Practice finding the sweet spot on the plunger markers during your first ball, then use that reference for balls 2 and 3. One effective tournament strategy is to too-short plunge on ball 1 to find the range, accepting you won't get the 100K, but then nail it on balls 2 and 3.

## Early Game Strategy
Focus on the **upper drop target bank** immediately after the skill shot. Completing this 3-bank scores 10K initially and increases both its own value and the value of the far left lane. Hit the **stand-up targets** to advance whether the drops are lit for X's or O's. The inlanes and slingshots also change the X/O designation, so use this to your advantage. Target the upper drops early as they advance left lane values up to **60K**.

## Mid Game Strategy
Work on completing tic-tac-toe patterns in the center grid. **Three in a row of the same symbol** (XXX or OOO) awards **20K in bonus**. A **mixed pattern** (combination of X's and O's) completing the card awards **90K in bonus**. Prioritize mixed patterns for higher bonus scoring. Use the **UTAD shots via left orbit** or towards the upper right flipper to access the upper drop targets consistently. The key is managing which symbol (X or O) is currently lit through strategic use of inlanes and stand-up targets.

## Late Game Strategy
Focus on **bonus collection** as this is where significant points come from. Ensure you complete tic-tac-toe patterns before losing the ball, as the 90K mixed pattern bonus can be game-changing. Be conservative near drain areas - this is an early solid-state game without modern safety features. **Trap up** when you have a good bonus built up and plan your final shots carefully.

## Multiballs
This early Bally solid-state machine does not feature multiball. Focus on single-ball scoring strategies and bonus maximization.

## Key Tips
- **Master the skill shot timing** - 100K is huge on this machine, practice the plunge strength
- **Upper drop targets are your bread and butter** - they increase lane values and their own completion value
- **Mixed tic-tac-toe patterns worth 90K** - prioritize these over three-in-a-row (20K)
- **Inlanes and slingshots change X/O designation** - use strategically to get the symbols you need
- **Left lane can be worth up to 60K** when advanced by upper drop completions
- **Nudge into right saucer** on right outlane drains to save the ball
- **Hard left nudge** on left inlane drains can save like in Fathom
- **Step up quickly** for skill shot - only 5-10 seconds before it times out
- **Avoid dangerous center shots** unless specifically going for pattern completion
